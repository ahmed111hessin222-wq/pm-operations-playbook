Cloud, Infrastructure & Admin Operations (OCI & Cloud)

An operational overview of multi-cloud account administration (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and Alibaba Cloud), server provisioning, VoIP/GSM hardware integration, and multi-partner infrastructure operations across healthcare and AI initiatives.

 Oracle / OCI Master Account & Server Provisioning
- Master Account Administration: Managing the primary company Oracle / OCI account hosting the majority of operational servers.
- Custom Server Provisioning:Creating and provisioning servers on Oracle tailored to partner technical requirements and operational workloads.
- Ongoing Infrastructure Administration: Overseeing server availability, operational stability, and day-to-day infrastructure administration across hosted environments.


MG Registry Multi-Partner Operations (KSA & UAE)
- Multi-Vendor Server Management: Administering dedicated servers for two distinct partner organizations—one based in India and one based in Egypt—collaborating on the MG Registry platform.
- Regional Healthcare Registry Support: Supporting infrastructure operations for the platform tracking multiple sclerosis (MS) symptoms across Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates.
- Requirement-Aligned Setup: Configuring and maintaining server environments aligned with each partner's technical specifications and workflow needs.


 AI Voice Campaign Infrastructure (Alibaba Cloud & VoIP Integration)
- Alibaba Cloud & VoIP Server Provisioning: Setting up an Alibaba Cloud account and provisioning a dedicated (VoIP server) to support automated outbound AI voice campaigns for a healthcare education platform targeting physicians and healthcare practitioners in (Saudi Arabia).
- Hardware & GSM Gateway Integration:Procuring, configuring, and integrating a Dinstar GSM gateway equipped with a SIM card into the VoIP infrastructure to enable live PSTN call routing.
- Bilingual AI Voice Agent Enablement: Supporting the technical environment enabling an AI voice agent capable of conducting full, interactive marketing and outreach calls in both (Arabic) and (English).


 Engineering & Stakeholder Governance
- Technical Coordination: Coordinating with database and server engineers across hosted environments to maintain system stability and readiness.
- Regulatory & Security Alignment: Aligning infrastructure operations with project owners and governance entities (SDAIA and NCA) on compliance and data security standards.
