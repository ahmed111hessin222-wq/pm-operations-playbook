# Technical Support Leadership & Systems Operations

An overview of the field technical support workflows, call triage structure, and operational systems managed to maintain continuous execution across nationwide survey operations.

---

## Field Support Workflow and Ticketing System
- **Initial Ticketing Management:** Managing support requests through the **Jira** ticketing system before transitioning to direct phone communication for field issues requiring immediate intervention.
- **Live Field Support:** Providing direct phone support to interviewers and researchers while they are inside participants' homes during interviews or surveys to resolve issues quickly.
- **End-of-Day Documentation:** Recording and documenting tickets in **Jira** at the end of the day to ensure clear and traceable records.

---

## Call Triage & Task Distribution
- **Centralized Call Reception:** Designating a central point of contact to receive all incoming calls from the field.
- **Issue Classification & Routing:** Classifying and routing each issue to the appropriate team based on its type:
  - **Technical issues** routed to the **IT team**.
  - **Logistical issues** routed to the **Logistics team**.
  - **Administrative issues** routed to the **Administrative team**.

---

## Core Systems & Tools Managed
- **Survey Solutions:** Real-time field survey monitoring, synchronization tracking, and live troubleshooting.
- **Jira:** End-of-day incident logging, ticket documentation, and issue tracking.
- **Oracle:** Operational database administration and data synchronization oversight.
- **Call Center & WhatsApp Business:** Phone interview routing, inbound support lines, and beneficiary appointment scheduling and rescheduling workflows.

---

## Oracle Coordination, Governance & Stakeholders
- **Technical Follow-up with Oracle Engineers:** Coordinating with **Oracle** database and server engineers to follow up on system stability, infrastructure readiness, and technical operations.
- **Project Owner Alignment:** Attending meetings with project owners to align on technical updates, operational requirements, and execution status.
- **Regulatory & Cybersecurity Coordination:** Coordinating with **SDAIA** and the **National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)** on compliance, data governance, and cybersecurity alignment.
