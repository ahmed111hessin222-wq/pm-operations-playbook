# PM & Operations Playbook

A structured portfolio and operational reference highlighting hands-on experience across **Project Management**, **Technical Support Leadership**, and **Oracle / OCI Account & Admin Operations**.

## Core Role Tracks

### 1. Project Management
End-to-end project coordination, operational planning, stakeholder and vendor alignment, and delivery tracking across multi-track initiatives.

### 2. Technical Support Lead
Leading technical support operations, managing incident and ticketing workflows across multiple systems, and ensuring smooth day-to-day operational continuity.

### 3. Oracle / OCI Account & Admin Operations
Managing Oracle / OCI account coordination, technical and commercial alignment, and structured administrative and operational follow-ups.
