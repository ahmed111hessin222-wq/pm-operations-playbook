# Projects Overview

A summary of key nationwide survey and operational initiatives managed and supported across **Project Management**, **Field Technical Support Leadership**, and **System/Database Operations**.

---

 1. Saudi World Health Survey (WHS)
Role: Field Technical Support Lead & Operations Coordinator

- Nationwide Field Support & Logistics: Led the technical support team to resolve real-time technical issues and field logistics across numerous survey teams operating simultaneously across all regions of Saudi Arabia.
- Field Documentation & Progress Reporting: Prepared and delivered structured daily and weekly field progress reports, supported by operational and instructional documentation videos to standardize field execution.
- Database & Server Administration: Managed operational data on Oracle databases and oversaw server stability to ensure continuous data synchronization and system availability.

---

 2. Saudi National Health and Nutrition Survey (S-NHANES)
Role: Project & Operations Coordinator / Technical Support Lead

- Multi-Stage Survey Workflow Coordination: Managed operational continuity across a three-stage beneficiary journey—linking household interviews, mobile clinic appointment scheduling, and follow-up phone nutrition interviews.
- Call Center Setup & Phone Interview Operations: Coordinated call center routing and technical readiness to support outbound nutrition phone interviews and inbound beneficiary inquiries.
- WhatsApp Business Channel Management: Structured and managed WhatsApp Business workflows to handle appointment rescheduling requests and streamline participant communication.
